# Photos for Nadia's site: what to do in Gemini

The ad at `/jumpstart/how-it-works` and the example site at `/jumpstart/how-it-works/example` use patterned placeholders where the photos go. Gemini makes the four real photos. Claude does the rest.

## Your steps

1.  Open the Gemini app (gemini.google.com) and start a new chat.
2.  Copy **Photo 1** below, paste it in, and send.
3.  If it looks right, download it. If not, type "try again" or say what to fix.
4.  Do the same for Photos 2, 3, and 4, in the same chat.
5.  Leave the four downloads in your Downloads folder, and tell Claude "the photos are in Downloads".

Claude then finds them, resizes them, puts them in the site, and shows you the result. You do not need to rename anything.

A good photo has no writing or logos anywhere, fabric that looks real, and normal-looking hands.
