# Jumpstart materials, 2026-11-10

The founder: "the material we have on it is sorely lacking. that includes the site, the helper material like 1-pager, quick start" and "if a founder was to say, make me a website, how would this play out?"

## What changed

*   **New page `/jumpstart/how-it-works`**: "Make me a website", played as eight scenes (about a minute), autoplaying when on screen, with pause and scene buttons, and a storyboard instead of motion for readers who prefer reduced motion. Every scene follows the traced flow in `founder-course/jumpstart/review/audits/2026-11-10-website-flow.md`: the website skill is in every install; the build kit is for builders and is offered as a Decide; the route is a Decide on the dashboard; gaps are marked `[NEED: ...]`; the founder buys the domain and publishes.
*   **One-pager (`/jumpstart/mustech-os` and its PDF)**: rewritten around what the product does today and what the research found. The four tabs are now customers, money and admin, a website, and every day. Removed claims that were not true for every founder: automatic checks across every page (needs the build kit), "pricing recommendations" (the founder's rule: never advise on prices), "S$0/mo" (a subscription exists for packages and updates; replaced by "nothing breaks if you stop paying"), "82 playbooks" (now 92 skills, installed to fit), and "2 half-days" (now "two days, in person", matching the main page's "two days, three hours a day"). The standup sample is a mix of trades. Gates are described plainly.
*   **Quick start (`/jumpstart/quick-start` and its PDF)**: plain step titles, no labels above headings, "Windows 11" instead of a wrong ".msi", the setup email "sent to the address you signed up with", no promise of a "1-year licence" on payment, and a link to the explainer.
*   **Main page (`/jumpstart`)**: four labels above headings removed (the founder's rule); a research section, "Built on what owners asked AI for", with the six most-asked items, how often each came up, and what MusTech OS does about each; the safety section now explains gates and what never gets one.

## Research used

From `founder-course/jumpstart/review/audits/2026-11-09-demand-research.md` (Gemini Deep Research, folded in 2026-11-09): 58 asks from 189 mentions; S23 listings 8, S25 repeat questions 8, S28 missed calls 6, S29 receipts 6, S30 PayNow matching 5, S31 quotes 5, S37 grants 4; and two findings: owners want read-only views of money and a yes before a message goes, and they stopped trusting AI over made-up facts and machine-sounding writing. The report says the counts are a ranking, not exact numbers; the page does not claim more.
